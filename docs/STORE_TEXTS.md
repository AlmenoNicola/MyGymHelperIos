## Note per la revisione (App Review Information)
The app counts repetitions with the iPhone motion sensors: strap the phone to your arm or leg (or hold it firmly)
and do a few curls after tapping "Start"; the first 3 seconds calibrate the movement. Subscriptions can be tested
with the sandbox account [indicare]. An account is optional (Info > Account) and is not required to purchase.
Health data stays on device; Apple Health is optional (Info > Connect Apple Health).
